# Sutra 2: People First

## Why does it matter?

AI should support people, not replace human judgment. In financial services, that means AI can help improve speed, efficiency and decision-making, but the final call should remain with humans, especially where safety, fairness or public interest is involved.

People should also know when they are interacting with AI, and they should be protected from confusion, misuse and harm. If AI is used well, it can make services better for citizens. If used carelessly, it can make people feel powerless, misled or left out.

## What would this look like in practice?

RBI translates the People First Sutra into practical measures through recommendations including:

1. **Recommendation 8 — AI Liability Framework:** REs remain liable for losses suffered by customers.
2. **Recommendation 10 — Capacity Building within REs:** Boards, senior management and employees should develop the knowledge needed to use AI ethically and responsibly.
3. **Recommendation 14 — Board Approved AI Policy:** Require each institution to have a Board-approved AI policy including measures to protect customers.
4. **Recommendation 16 — AI System Governance Framework:** Autonomous and higher-risk AI systems should have appropriate human oversight.
5. **Recommendation 18 — Consumer Protection:** Customers should receive transparency, fairness, accessible recourse, education about safe AI use, and information about their rights.

*Note: Recommendation numbers and titles follow the RBI FREE-AI Committee Report. The content under this Sutra captures only the parts that support the People First principle; the mapping is my interpretation.*
