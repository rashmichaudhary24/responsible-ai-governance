# Sutra 3: Innovation over Restraint

## Why does it matter?

AI can help financial institutions improve their products, services and ways of working. RBI believes that **responsible innovation should be encouraged, not held back simply because AI carries risks**.

Where responsible innovation and cautionary restraint are both possible, and all other things are equal, **responsible innovation should be preferred**. The aim is therefore not to ignore AI risks, but to manage them without unnecessarily preventing useful innovation.

## What would this look like in practice?

RBI translates the Innovation over Restraint Sutra into practical measures through:

1. **Recommendation 2 — AI Innovation Sandbox:** Enable REs, FinTechs and other innovators to develop AI-driven solutions, algorithms and models in a secure and controlled environment.
2. **Recommendation 3 — Incentives and Funding Support:** Put incentives and infrastructure in place to encourage inclusive and equitable AI use among smaller entities, and consider funding for data and compute infrastructure to support innovation and strategic sectoral needs.
3. **Recommendation 4 — Indigenous Financial Sector Specific AI Models:** Develop financial-sector-specific AI models, including LLMs, SLMs and non-LLM models, and offer them as a public good.
4. **Recommendation 6 — Adaptive and Enabling Policies:** Ensure that existing policies and legal frameworks enable AI-driven innovation, and develop flexible, forward-looking guidance for AI innovation and adoption.
5. **Recommendation 7 — Enabling AI-Based Affirmative Action:** Encourage AI-driven innovation that accelerates financial inclusion of underserved and unserved sections of society.
6. **Recommendation 8 — AI Liability Framework:** Adopt a graded liability framework that **encourages responsible innovation**, with a more accommodating supervisory approach where appropriate safety mechanisms have been followed.
7. **Recommendation 9 — AI Institutional Framework:** Establish a mechanism to continuously advise on **emerging opportunities** and risks and monitor the evolution of AI technology.
8. **Recommendation 12 — Framework for Sharing Best Practices:** Promote responsible scaling by sharing AI use cases, lessons learned, positive outcomes, challenges and sound governance practices.
9. **Recommendation 13 — Recognise and Reward Responsible AI Innovation:** Recognise and reward responsible AI innovation, particularly where it demonstrates positive social impact and embeds ethical considerations by design.

*Note: Recommendation numbers and titles follow the RBI FREE-AI Committee Report. The content under this Sutra is a focused extraction of the relevant part of each recommendation; the mapping of recommendations to the Seven Sutras is my interpretation.*
