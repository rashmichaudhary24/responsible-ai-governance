# Sutra 5: Accountability

## Why does it matter?

The organisation that uses AI remains responsible for what the AI does. It cannot pass that responsibility to the AI system, the model or the algorithm behind it.

As AI becomes more automated, it becomes even more important to know **who is responsible, who answers when something goes wrong, and how that responsibility can be demonstrated.**

## What would this look like in practice?

RBI translates the Accountability Sutra into practical measures through:

1. **Recommendation 8 — AI Liability Framework:** REs remain responsible for any loss suffered by customers. A more accommodating supervisory approach should apply only when appropriate safety measures have been followed, and not when there are repeated breaches, serious negligence or failure to fix known problems.
2. **Recommendation 10 — Capacity Building within REs:** Boards, senior management and employees should develop the knowledge and skills needed to govern AI and manage its risks.
3. **Recommendation 14 — Board Approved AI Policy:** Establish a Board-approved AI policy that clearly sets out **governance, accountability, risk appetite, safeguards, auditability and liability.**
4. **Recommendation 15 — Data Lifecycle Governance:** Put **clear internal controls and policies** in place for how AI-related data is collected, accessed, used, stored and deleted.
5. **Recommendation 16 — AI System Governance Framework:** Put in place **clear governance across the entire AI lifecycle**, including documentation, validation and ongoing monitoring.
6. **Recommendation 22 — AI Incident Reporting and Sectoral Risk Intelligence Framework:** Create a system for **detecting and reporting AI incidents** and encourage timely reporting.
7. **Recommendation 23 — AI Inventory within REs and Sector-Wide Repository:** Maintain an internal record of **all AI models and use cases**, including their risks and dependencies, and make it available for inspection and audit.
8. **Recommendation 24 — AI Audit Framework:** Regularly **check how AI is being governed**, including independent audits for high-risk or complex AI systems.
9. **Recommendation 25 — Disclosures by REs:** Require institutions to **disclose relevant information about their use of AI** in their annual reports and on their websites.
10. **Recommendation 26 — AI Toolkit:** Help institutions **check and demonstrate** whether they are following responsible AI principles, including accountability.

*Note: Recommendation numbers and titles follow the RBI FREE-AI Committee Report. The content under this Sutra is a focused extraction of the relevant part of each recommendation; the mapping of recommendations to the Seven Sutras is my interpretation.*
