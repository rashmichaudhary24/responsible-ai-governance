# Sutra 6: Understandable by Design

## Why does it matter?

AI systems can be difficult to understand, especially when they produce decisions or recommendations without clearly showing how they arrived at them.

RBI therefore treats understandability as something that should be built into AI from the start. The entities deploying AI should be able to understand how it works and what its outputs mean, and people should be told when AI is being used.

## What would this look like in practice?

RBI translates the Understandable by Design Sutra into practical measures through:

1. **Recommendation 16 — AI System Governance Framework:** Maintain **model documentation and validation** across the AI lifecycle so that the AI systems being deployed can be properly understood and monitored.
2. **Recommendation 18 — Consumer Protection:** Prioritise **transparency** and educate customers about safe AI use and their rights.
3. **Recommendation 25 — Disclosures by REs:** Include **AI-related disclosures** in annual reports and websites, with a consistent AI-specific disclosure framework across institutions.
4. **Recommendation 26 — AI Toolkit:** Help REs validate and demonstrate compliance with responsible AI principles including **transparency**.

*Note: Recommendation numbers and titles follow the RBI FREE-AI Committee Report. The content under this Sutra is a focused extraction of the relevant part of each recommendation; the mapping of recommendations to the Seven Sutras is my interpretation.*
